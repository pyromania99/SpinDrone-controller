## Why `fast_circle_l1.csv` exists

`fast_circle_l2.csv` starts its cosine ramp at t = 0. At levels 2 and 3 that is fine
because `fly.py` takes off, reaches altitude and spins up *before* streaming it. Level 1's
`--trajectory` starts streaming immediately, with the vehicle at 1.6 m and spinning through
9 rad/s, so it enters the circle already 4 m behind and never recovers — it is not a
controller failure, it is a staging failure.

[`logs/fast_circle_l1.csv`](logs/fast_circle_l1.csv) is that file with its first point held
for **15 s**, generated by:

```python
rows = [l.split(",") for l in open("examples/results/fast_circle_l2.csv").read().strip().split("\n")]
t0, x0, y0, z0 = rows[0]
out  = [f"{i*0.02:.5f},{float(x0):.5f},{float(y0):.5f},{float(z0):.5f}" for i in range(750)]
out += [f"{float(t)+15:.5f},{float(x):.5f},{float(y):.5f},{float(z):.5f}" for t, x, y, z in rows]
```

The flown path is unchanged; only the entry differs. With it, all four arms hold 6.00 m at
zero follow error until the circle starts at t = 15 s.

## THE TRAP that cost this session several hours

**`--max-lateral-accel` defaults to disabled.** Not to 3.0, not to anything — the
controller's default is `0.0` and the clamp is guarded by `if self.max_lateral_accel > 0.0`.
Every level-1 trajectory flight in this tree uses the clamp, and nothing enforces or warns.

Runs flown **without** it on 2026-09-18, all of which are void and are **not** kept in this
folder:

| arm | no clamp | with clamp |
|---|---|---|
| fast circle, seat OFF | crash 13.1 s into the circle | flies 65 s |
| fast circle, seat ON, k = 0.02 | crash 8.1 s | flies 65 s, better tracking |
| fast circle, seat ON, k = 0.2 | crash 5.2 s | not flown |
| fast circle, seat ON, k = 2.0 (default) | crash 3.9 s, θ_s railed 74.6 % | flies (0.0 % railed) |
| fast circle, seat ON + adaptive b | lost the hover at 4.9 s, before the circle | not flown |

Those produced a clean-looking monotonic gain sweep and an apparent finding that the seat
is harmful at level 1 on circles. **Both were artifacts of the missing clamp.** The θ_s
railing was too: the clamped runs rail on 0.0 % of ticks.
