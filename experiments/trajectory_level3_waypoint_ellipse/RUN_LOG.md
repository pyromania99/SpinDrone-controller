# Run log — trajectory, level 3, waypoint ellipse, 20 ms rotor pole

Flown 2026-09-20. **The first setpoint-tracking runs at level 3** — the tree had only hover,
rotor-loss and the fast circle before this.

These sit outside [`../phase_only/`](../phase_only/) because they post-date it, but they are
directly comparable to it: the magnitude term that briefly existed on 2026-09-19 was reverted
the same day, and `MC_SEAT_SATGATE = 1` is the behaviour every archived run was flown with.

**NEITHER CRASHED.** `nav_state` goes 14 (OFFBOARD) → 18 (`AUTO_LAND`) at ~75 s and the
altitude walks down from there; `land_detected` flips at 79.4 s. A naive "altitude below 1 m
after being above 3 m" test calls that a crash — it is not one. Score these on `nav_state`,
not on altitude.

`tau_eff` is measured the same way as everything else in the tree (cross-spectrum phase of
torque-command → measured angular acceleration at the spin frequency, 30 Welch segments,
coherence 0.74–0.92) and lands where the archived hover runs at the same `rotor_tau` do
(39.2–39.9 ms). Same plant, different profile.

## What this shows

**A 20 ms rotor pole does not kill setpoint tracking the way it kills the circle.** The
fast circle at this pole dies at r = 25.8 on both arms, and
[`../phase_only/analysis/SESSION_2026-09-18.md`](../phase_only/analysis/SESSION_2026-09-18.md)
§2d records that a 6× reduction in centripetal demand changes nothing. The difference here is
not speed — it is that advancing on ARRIVAL removes the *sustained* tilt demand. Demand p95
sits at 21–32 % of the real authority ceiling where the fast circle exceeds it on 40 % of
ticks.

**Demand grows faster than spin.** A 19 % spin increase produced a **49 % rise in p95 `|xd|`**
(11.8 → 17.6 rad/s²), an exponent near 2.3. That is the superlinear growth §3c measured as 23×
between flying and failing arms and could not derive — visible here in a clean, unsaturated
regime instead of inside a crash. Extrapolating, the real ceiling would arrive near
**r ≈ 32–34**, which this profile can test cheaply.

**θ_s barely moved, and its scatter doubled.** −0.447 → −0.473 for a 19 % spin increase, where
the model wants it to grow with Ω, while sd went 0.106 → 0.232. Consistent with the
under-convergence seen elsewhere: measured against the phase actually present in the log, θ_s
reaches 0.39–0.63 of it whenever a rotor pole is present.

## Caveats

- **n = 1 per cell.** The level-2 circle rescue looked equally clean at n = 1 and turned out
  to be 1-in-4. Repeat both before quoting.
- Following distance 1.10 m against a 1.29 m median leg is roughly one leg length, not tight
  tracking. The p95 of 2.13 m says it overshoots the longer legs.
- `plot_run.py`'s crash-cut assumes the 6 m hover profile and truncates these at ~18 s. The
  committed figures were generated with `--no-crash-cut`; keep that flag for this profile.
